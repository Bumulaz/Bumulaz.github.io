Part 6 • Explain Your Design Choices

My Color Analysis

Explain how your color choices relate to color theory and how you would use each palette in a website.

Palette 1

Harmony: Complementary

Why I selected these colors: I liked the muted color that this palette brings to the website. The Primary and Secondary colors are close to each other in terms of contrast and it feels right. The brown accent on the buttons sort of breaks this harmony in order to bring attention to itself

Mood: This brings a mellow feeling to the website, and yet still bright enough to be a website that holds important info like for example, say a medical website or something.

Website Usage: The Primary color I would use for all the most important things on a website like using it as a background since it is a very prominent hue. The accent color for buttons and perhaps some text cards. Secondary color I would use for buttons that are prominent and require more attention than the others that I chose the accent color for.

Palette 2

Harmony: Analogous

Why I selected these colors: I really liked the bright and vibrant colors that this palette gives, without being far too bright and neon

Mood: A strong palette with a friendly feeling.

Website Usage: I would use the surface color as a backround on certain parts of the website. The secondary color is something that I can see being used as a navbar.

Palette 3

Harmony: Triadic

Why I selected these colors: I really liked how harmonious the green colors were to each other and it seemed really perfect.

Mood: A very calm and laid back palette giving a natural foresty vibe.

Website Usage: I would use the background behind cards or info fields on a website. The Primary color would encompass the bulk of the website since the hue of the color allows most text colors to be visible within it. The secondary color could be a nav bar color that would be inside a hamburger menu.
