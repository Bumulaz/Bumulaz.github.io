Jazz Image Editing Practice

The images below will show me practicing some editing techniques on some jazz images to change the overall feeling and mood that they give.

Saxophonist Noir

Below we see the before and after of the saxophone player. Original image has the player in golden light playing amidst smoke on a stage.
In the edited image, I went for a classic black and white image. A throwback reminding us of retro 1950's-esque times.
The original image gives sort of a triumphant feeling with the golden colors that it had, the latter though gives sort of a more classic feeling and dreamlike feeling, as if it were something that you see within a memory.

A Band Brought To Light

The original image shows a band as they are beginning their rehearsal for their upcoming show. The lighting is good, though I felt that it could be better.
I added some saturation to the image to give it a bit more oomph so that if there was any question of the original image not being clear, then it certainly is now.

A Record of Time

Here was a cute image that I saw while walking in a record store the other day. I decided to snap a picture of it.
I changed the contrast of the image so that there was a bit more of a grainy look to the picture. It sort of makes it look like it has some film grain which I felt was fitting with the color that the image has. Sort of gives it a warmer and nostalgic feeling.

Edited Image

From Gold to Sky

With the main instruments of jazz being made of brass, you are going to see alot of gold.
I decided I wanted to see what would it feel like if I changed that to a very opposite color to that, something like a nice blue.
I had a picture of a closeup of a saxophonist playing, and added a monochrome blue theme to it. It is a bit of a loud color but it makes it look like he went from performing in a concerto to just a normal concert that you can catch at your local music hall with the blue light straight on him.

Photography

The photos that were taken were all related to jazz artists and their instruments. Jazz brings a warm and soothing feeling which invites me to do well when editing.

Inspiration

Jazz has a calming and also upbeat environment, it is a very versatile genre of music and is one of the best things to put on when working, so doing work around it felt like the right thing to do.

Creativity

Editing images allows you to tweak images, videos, or even webpages to tailor it to be as perfect as you would like it to be. It is an amazing skill to have as you are able to turn something that originally looks mundane or incomplete, into something that really stands out to people or whoever is seeing your work.
